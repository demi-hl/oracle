Custody

The wallet stays yours.

bound grants

Wallet with temporary agent permission

Max value, chain, venue, destinations, and TTL. Outside the grant = hard refuse, not an agent with a free wallet.

bounded surfaces

Six lanes, each with its own decoder

hl, poly, evm-swap, evm-bridge, btc, sol. Each decodes its own envelope, proves every value-moving field, and refuses while its allowlists are empty.

evidence

Decoded before you sign, receipted after

minOut, impact, spender, and decoded calldata before approval; tx hash, receipt status, balance delta, and allowance state after. Unsigned artifacts export to hardware wallets. No “looks filled.”

fail closed

Unconfigured means refused

A fresh install signs nothing. Destination allowlists stop silent router drift, and every lane refuses while its allowlists are empty, so new contracts stay blocked until you add them.
