Self-custodial agents

No third party holds your keys. Not even Oracle.

Everything runs on your machine

The CLI and the desktop app are the whole product. There is no Oracle account, no hosted wallet, and no server that brokers your trades. Reads go straight from your machine to public RPCs and venue APIs.

Your keys never reach Oracle

The shipped package contains no signer, no vault, and no key material. Whatever key you use stays where you already keep it, in your wallet extension or on your hardware device, under that tool’s own protection. A boundary test walks the real import graph on every build and fails if a public module so much as reaches toward signer code.

Your wallet signs, not Oracle

Oracle prepares an unsigned transaction and hands it to your browser extension or hardware wallet. It cannot broadcast. If you close the prompt, nothing happened.

Nothing to seize, nothing to leak

No custody means no honeypot. There is no server holding user keys to breach, no operator who can freeze you, and no counterparty risk from Oracle itself. Delete the app and your funds never moved.

Agentic strategies

Move liquidity. Bound the risk.

Oracle reads the position, models the route and costs, then prepares only the steps the selected lane can actually support.

LP rebalancing

Read concentrated-liquidity positions, model new ranges and fee drag, then prepare collect, swap and redeploy steps on supported venues. You sign each step.

MEV

Monitor cross-venue divergence and simulate net edge after gas, slippage and lender costs. Public mode is shadow analysis, not autonomous execution.

simulate · policy-check

Flash loans

Build bounded atomic loan routes and test premium, calldata, gas and minimum-profit gates. Public live execution remains disabled.

Staking

Compare validators, staking vaults, rewards and unlock periods, then prepare supported stake, withdraw and delegate actions for your signature.

discover · compare · prepare

Yield

Rank staking, vault and LP opportunities by net APY after fees, liquidity, lockups and smart-contract risk, then prepare supported deposits or migrations for your signature.

model · hedge · prepare

Farming methods

Six delta-aware playbooks, from stablecoin loops to collateral-plus-perp hedges and LP incentive hedges. Each one carries explicit exposure, hedge, monitoring and exit rules rather than a bare APR.

hedge · fund · rebalance

Delta neutral

Pair a spot or LP leg against a short perp so the position earns funding and fees without directional exposure. Oracle reads both legs, models funding, borrow and rebalance drag, then prepares each leg for your signature.

split · schedule · prepare

TWAP accumulate & distribute

Buy or sell over time instead of in one print. Oracle splits the notional into scheduled child orders, prices each against live venue depth, and prepares them individually so a single fill never moves the market against you. Same engine for distribution — size each tranche, cap participation, keep every child order unsigned.

route · revoke · bridge

deBridge · ChangeNOW · LI.FI · revoke.cash

Bridge and cross-chain integration with deBridge, ChangeNOW, LI.FI, Squid, Stargate, THORChain, Bungee, Connext, Wormhole, and Across. Every route checks revoke.cash before execution — Oracle prepares the unsigned tx, you sign.

discover · score · rank

Live farm discovery

Scan live pools across chains and score each candidate on net APR after estimated borrow, funding and gas, minus a risk haircut for thin TVL. Ranked candidates feed straight into a prepare plan.

alert · shadow · notify

Custom alerts + shadow trading

Follow favorite chains, wallets, funds and deployer addresses, then get notified when one opens, rotates or exits. Run the playbook in shadow mode against live prices before you arm it for live execution.

shadow · simulate · execute

Paper-trade until you go live

Simulate every strategy against live venue data without committing capital. Track P&L, slippage, and fill quality in shadow mode — then arm it with one flag when you're ready. No gas, no exposure, no regret trades.

Public posture: research, simulation and unsigned preparation. MEV and flash-loan broadcast stays off; supported staking, LP, yield and farming actions require the user wallet to sign. Fees

10 bps. Holders zero.

Oracle takes 10 basis points (0.10%) on swaps it routes. No subscription, no per-chain surcharge, no hidden spread. One flat rate across every venue.

swap fee

10 bps per routed swap

Calculated on the output amount, deducted from the route before the quote reaches you. Oracle never touches your input — it prices the net you receive after the fee.

locals only

0% for NFT holders

The Locals Only NFT is the license. Hold it in the wallet Oracle reads from and every swap routes at zero Oracle fees. The CLI recognizes the holder wallet automatically — no claim, no dashboard, no manual toggle.

operator seat

Run your own instance

The public package is free to install. Every read, route, and prepare runs on your machine. You keep 100% of the value you generate — the 10 bps only applies when your agent routes through Oracle's aggregation.

builder codes

Hyperliquid builder fees

Eligible Hyperliquid orders can include a builder fee. Your wallet approves the builder and maximum rate, and Oracle shows the fee before you sign. This is separate from the 10 bps routed-swap fee.
